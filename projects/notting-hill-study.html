Design concept

A calm intellectual refuge tucked within a five-storey stucco Victorian townhouse. We lined the private study in solid American black walnut book shelving with concealed acoustic linen dampening, framing quiet north-facing windows. Deep bronze accents anchor the writing desk against an open limestone fireplace.
