Design concept

Maximizing the sense of volume within a historic Chiswick mews house. We introduced a central double-height lightwell that channels zenithal daylight directly onto a monolithic Roman travertine kitchen counter. Limewashed walls absorb and gently diffuse the southern exposure throughout the sunken ground-floor living pavilion.
