Design concept

A 19th-century tannery warehouse converted into a sanctuary of raw materiality and quiet minimalism. Rather than subdividing the open expanse with solid walls, we introduced floor-to-ceiling blackened steel and fluted glass partitions, allowing morning light to wash continuously across polished micro-cement and exposed London stock brick.
