Design concept

Dissolving the boundary between domestic interior and an English garden. A low-profile structural steel and glass pavilion cantilevers out from the original brick façade. Flush terrazzo thresholds create an unbroken datum extending straight from the open kitchen to the outdoor dining terrace.
