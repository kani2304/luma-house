Clerkenwell, London · Founded 2014

We started Luma House in 2014 with one conviction: that a home should be designed around how light and material behave, not around what’s fashionable.

Ten years on, that hasn’t changed. Every project begins the same way — by listening to how a family actually lives, then building a home around that, one considered decision at a time. We’re based in Clerkenwell, working across London and the home counties on residential and architectural projects of every scale.

Principles
Less, but considered
Every element earns its presence. We don’t add until we understand what belongs.
Materials over finishes
We specify for how things age, not how they photograph on the day they’re completed.
Light as the first material
We design the light in a room before we design anything that sits inside it.
